What CalMobile is

CalMobile is an app to log what you eat and follow your calories and macros. By using it you accept these terms. If you do not agree, do not use it. How we handle your data is in the privacy policy.

It is not medical advice

CalMobile is an informational tool. Its targets and calculations are estimates and do not replace the advice of a doctor, dietitian or nutritionist. If you have an illness or an eating disorder, are pregnant or take medication, talk to a professional before changing your diet. Do not use the app to make medical decisions.

Food data can be wrong

Nutrition values come from public and crowd-sourced databases (USDA FoodData Central, Open Food Facts and data published by supermarkets and chains) and from recipes calculated from them. They may contain errors or be out of date. Check the product label if something does not add up.

  • Free trial. If it is the first time you subscribe, you get 14 days free. Plus is activated the moment you subscribe and nothing is charged during those 14 days. The first charge is made when the trial ends (day 15), for the amount that was shown to you. If you cancel in Google Play before then, you pay nothing.
  • Renewal and cancellation. The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period until you cancel it in Google Play → Payments and subscriptions. If you cancel, you keep Plus until the end of the period already paid.
  • Right of withdrawal. As a consumer you have 14 calendar days from the moment you subscribe to withdraw without giving a reason. That period coincides with the free trial: to withdraw, it is enough to cancel the subscription in Google Play (or write to us at the contact email) before it ends, and you are not charged. The right of withdrawal can be exercised only once: it does not start again when the subscription becomes a paid one or when it renews, because the price and the date of the first charge are shown to you before you subscribe. If you subscribe without a free trial, you have the same 14-day period and the refund is processed through Google Play.
